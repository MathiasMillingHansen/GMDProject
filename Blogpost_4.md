# Blog 4:

With the animations done for the player it was time to further the game with some level design. First of i wanted to make sure the player couldnt move off the map, there were two ways to do this. I could just add an invisible collider that ensured the player couldnt move past the collider, but i choose to instead make a "mountian" with collision that ensured the player couldnt move of the map. This looked way nicer. What i discovered then were how walls were handled. Since the mountain was a part of the "ground" tilemap layer which had the "Ground" tag, the side of the mountain were "sticky" which meant the player could get stuck in the side of the mountain and keep getting stuck if they held in the respective direction button. This also enabled them to "climb" the wall by getting stuck, technically hitting the "ground" tag which enables them to jump again which they could repeat over and over again. To fix this i added another tilemap called "Edge tilemap" Which will be used whenever there is an edge where the player is supposed to just glide down when they hit it instead of getting stuck. To do this i added another tag called edge which i assigned to the edge tilemap. Now the player could now longer jump when colliding with edges since its no longer a "ground" tag. This also lead to another discovery. Now the "corner blocks" that change the y level which are meant to be jumped up on still have the sticky properties. This meant the player could experience some jank when trying to jump up blocks, again by getting "stuck". To solve this i added another tilemap called "CornerBlocks" which only purpose is to solve this issue. They are completely identical to the ground tilemap except they have 0 friction so the player wouldnt get stuck when trying to jump on top of them. With this now solved i began lengthening the game level. I added more platforms, some obstacles and experimented with world generation making sure to keep the correct tiles in the correct tilemaps. I also created a "middleground" layer so i could add background elements like bushes, houses and trees so the game wouldnt be boring to look at.
